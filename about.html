About Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity awareness means understanding how to use the internet safely. Today, we use mobile phones, computers, and the internet for studying, shopping, banking, and social media. Because of this, our personal information such as passwords, photos, messages, and bank details can be in danger if we are not careful.

Cybercriminals use tricks like fake emails, fake links, viruses, and hacking to steal information. Cybersecurity awareness teaches us how to recognize these dangers and avoid them. It helps us learn good habits, such as using strong passwords, not clicking unknown links, and keeping software updated.

When people have cybersecurity awareness, they can protect their identity, money, and devices. It also helps organizations and schools stay safe from cyber attacks. In simple words, cybersecurity awareness is about being smart and careful online so we can use technology safely without fear.

Cybersecurity Awareness Points:

1. Protects personal information like passwords and photos

2. Helps avoid fake emails and phishing links

3. Keeps bank details and money safe

4. Teaches how to create strong passwords

5. Prevents viruses and malware attacks

6. Helps recognize unsafe websites

Cybercrime Cases in Nepal (2019–2025)

Evolution of Cyber Laws in Nepal

Nepal's journey in framing cyber laws began in the early 2000s, coinciding with the rise of the internet. The Electronic Transactions Act (ETA), 2006 (also known as the Cyber Law of Nepal) remains the cornerstone of Nepal's cyber legal framework. However, other laws, such as the Muluki Penal Code, 2074, Copyright Act, 2002, Telecommunications Act, 2053, and Individual Privacy Act, 2018, also contribute to Nepal's broader cyber law landscape.

The 12 Types of Cybercrime According to Nepal's Cyber Law

  1. Unauthorized Access (Hacking)

    Accessing someone's computer, network, or data without permission.

  2. Damage or Alteration of Computer Data/System

    Deleting, modifying, or destroying data or software.

  3. Publication of Illegal/Obscene Content

    Sharing pornography or offensive electronic material.

  4. Sending Offensive or False Messages

    Spreading threatening, harassing, or fake messages online.

  5. Cyber Fraud and Scams

    Tricks like phishing, fake investment schemes, or online payment fraud.

  6. Identity Theft & Impersonation

    Using someone's personal information or digital identity to deceive or commit fraud.

  7. Breach of Confidentiality/Privacy

    Sharing private or sensitive information without authorization.

  8. Cyberbullying and Online Harassment

    Abusive behavior, threats, blackmail, or harassment via electronic means.

  9. Distribution of Malware or Malicious Code

    Creating/spreading viruses, ransomware, or harmful software.

  10. Data Theft or Phishing Attacks

    Stealing login credentials, passwords, or financial information.

  11. Denial of Service (DoS) & System Disruption

    Attacks that block legitimate users from accessing systems.

  12. Cyberterrorism / Attacks on Critical Infrastructure

    Using digital tools to threaten public security or disrupt vital systems.
