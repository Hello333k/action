Cybersecurity Resources

These resources help you learn more about cybersecurity and stay safe online. They are trusted websites that provide guides, tips, and latest security updates

🛡️

Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC)

The Australian Cyber Security Centre is a government organization that helps people and businesses stay safe online. It gives clear advice on how to protect computers, mobile phones, and personal information. ACSC explains how to avoid scams, phishing emails, and cyber attacks. It also shares alerts about new online threats and what to do if someone is hacked.

🔐

CISA – Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Security Agency

CISA is a U.S. government agency that works to protect computer systems and important services like banks, hospitals, and internet networks. It teaches people how to secure their devices, create strong passwords, and protect data. CISA also provides simple guides for organizations to stop cyber attacks and respond safely if an attack happens.

💼

Kaspersky Resource Center

The Kaspersky Resource Center is an education website created by a cybersecurity company. It explains online threats like viruses, malware, ransomware, and spyware in simple words. This resource helps users understand how hackers attack systems and how antivirus software can protect devices. It is very helpful for beginners learning about cyber threats
