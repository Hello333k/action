Cybersecurity Awareness

Cybersecurity awareness means knowing how to use the internet safely. It helps protect your personal information like passwords, photos, and bank details from hackers and online threats.

By learning cybersecurity basics, users can avoid phishing emails, malware, fake websites, and online scams. Safe internet habits keep your data and devices secure.

Why Cybersecurity Awareness Is Important

  • 🛡️Protects personal data and passwords
  • 🚫Prevents online scams and phishing
  • 💻Keeps devices safe from viruses
  • 🌐Helps use the internet safely
  • 🔒Reduces risk of hacking
